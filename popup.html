📌 What's real vs. estimated

Days running is real — read straight from Meta's "Started running on" date.

Spend and views are estimates. Meta does not publish spend or impressions for ordinary commercial ads (only for political / social-issue ads). AdScope models them from days-live × your assumed budget, so tune the assumptions above to your market. Treat them as relative signals, not exact figures.
